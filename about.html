UPI QR Codes

About UPI QR Codes

UPI QR Codes is a free browser-based UPI QR code generator and UPI payment link generator for Indian payments. It helps users create UPI payment QR codes for shops, invoices, donations, collections, and fixed-amount payment requests.

What the Site Does

The site creates UPI payment request links in the browser and turns them into QR codes that can be scanned by compatible UPI apps. Users can download the QR code or QR card as PNG, JPG, SVG, or PDF.

What the Site Does Not Do

  • It does not process payments or hold funds.
  • It does not verify settlement, refunds, or payment status.
  • It does not collect or store the UPI ID, payee name, amount, note, generated QR code, or payment link entered in the generator.
  • It is not affiliated with NPCI, BHIM, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay, any bank, or any payment service provider.

Trust and Transparency

The generator is designed as a lightweight static web tool. The project repository is public at GitHub, so the page structure and front-end implementation can be inspected.

For UPI network background, see the official NPCI UPI product overview and BHIM UPI official website.
