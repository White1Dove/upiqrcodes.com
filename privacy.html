UPI QR Codes

Privacy Policy

UPI QR Codes provides a browser-based UPI QR Codes Generator. The QR code is generated locally in your browser from the information you enter on the page.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share your merchant name, UPI ID, amount, note, generated QR code, or UPI payment link.

Third-Party Services

The site loads a QR code JavaScript library from cdnjs. Your entered payment details are not sent to that library provider by the application logic.

Payments

The site does not process payments, confirm payment status, handle refunds, or access your bank account. The payer approves payments inside their own UPI app.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. Last updated: May 7, 2026.
