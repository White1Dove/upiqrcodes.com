Privacy Policy

UPI QR Codes provides a browser-based UPI QR Code Generator. The QR code is generated locally in your browser from the information you enter on the page.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share your merchant name, UPI ID, amount, note, generated QR code, or UPI payment link.

Third-Party Services

The site loads a QR code JavaScript library from cdnjs. Your entered payment details are not sent to that library provider by the application logic.

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic, such as page views, traffic sources, country, device type, and browser. Google Analytics is not used to collect the UPI ID, merchant name, amount, note, generated QR code, or UPI payment link you enter in the generator.

Payments

The site does not process payments, confirm payment status, handle refunds, or access your bank account. The payer approves payments inside their own UPI app.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact support@upiqrcodes.com.
