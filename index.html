What is UPI QR Codes?

UPI QR Codes is a free browser-based UPI QR code generator for creating Indian UPI payment QR codes and UPI payment links. It is a front-end tool: it generates a UPI payment request link, but it does not process money, verify settlement, or act as a wallet.

A UPI QR code refers to a QR code that encodes a UPI payment request, usually including a payee UPI ID, payee name, INR currency, and optional amount or note.

UPI QR Code use cases

A UPI QR Code can support more than one payment flow. Choose a blank amount when the customer should type the value, or include an amount when the payment request is tied to a specific bill or invoice.

Shop counter QR

Create a static UPI QR Card with your shop name and UPI ID, print it, and let customers enter the amount after scanning.

Invoice payment QR

Add the invoice amount and a short note so the customer sees the payment context before approving in their UPI app.

Donation or collection QR

Leave the amount blank for flexible contributions, or set an amount for a fixed campaign, event, or collection request.

Static UPI QR without amount

Best when the payer should decide or type the amount. This is common for shop counters, stalls, donations, and repeated collections.

UPI QR with fixed amount

Best when the amount is already known. This works well for invoices, order totals, class fees, bookings, and one-time payment requests.

UPI QR FAQ

What is encoded in the UPI QR code?

The QR stores a UPI payment link such as upi://pay with fields for UPI ID, merchant name, INR currency, optional amount, and remarks.

Is this UPI QR Code Generator 100% free?

Yes. The tool is 100% free to use. You can generate, download, print, or share a UPI QR Code without creating an account.

Can I generate a QR without an amount?

Yes. Leave Amount blank to create a valid static UPI QR. The payer enters the amount inside their UPI app after scanning.

Can I download the UPI QR Code?

Yes. You can download the full QR Card or the QR code only in PNG, JPG, SVG, or PDF format.

Can I use this for a shop counter?

Yes. Enter your shop or payee name and UPI ID, leave the amount blank, generate the QR, and print the QR Card. Customers can scan it and enter the amount in their UPI app.

Can I create a UPI QR Code with a fixed amount?

Yes. Enter an amount before generating the QR. The generated UPI payment link includes the amount field so the payer sees that amount after scanning.

Does this confirm that money was received?

No. A browser-only QR generator creates the payment request. To confirm payment status automatically, connect a bank, PSP, or payment gateway API.

Is this a payment gateway?

No. This site is only a UPI QR Code and UPI payment link generator. It does not process money, hold funds, verify settlement, or provide refunds.

Is the UPI PIN included in the QR?

No. The payer enters the UPI PIN only inside their own UPI app while approving payment.
