UPI QR FAQ

What is encoded in the UPI QR code?

The QR stores a UPI payment link such as upi://pay with fields for UPI ID, merchant name, INR currency, optional amount, and remarks.

Is this UPI QR Codes Generator 100% free?

Yes. The tool is 100% free to use. You can generate, download, print, or share a UPI QR Code without creating an account.

Can I generate a QR without an amount?

Yes. Leave Amount blank to create a valid static UPI QR. The payer enters the amount inside their UPI app after scanning.

Can I download the UPI QR Code?

Yes. You can download the full QR Card or the QR code only in PNG, JPG, SVG, or PDF format.

Does this confirm that money was received?

No. A browser-only QR generator creates the payment request. To confirm payment status automatically, connect a bank, PSP, or payment gateway API.

Is the UPI PIN included in the QR?

No. The payer enters the UPI PIN only inside their own UPI app while approving payment.
