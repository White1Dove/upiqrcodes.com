What is a UPI payment link?

A UPI payment link is a URI that starts with upi://pay and includes payment request fields. Common fields include pa for payee address, pn for payee name, am for amount, tn for transaction note, and cu for currency.

When a compatible UPI app opens the link, it shows the payment request to the payer. The payer still reviews the details and approves the payment inside their own app.

Payment link vs UPI QR code

A UPI QR code is usually the same payment link encoded as a scannable QR. Use a direct UPI payment link when the payer is already on a phone. Use a QR code when the payer scans from another device or a printed card.

This is not a payment gateway. It cannot confirm success, handle refunds, or reconcile settlement.

UPI payment link FAQ

What does upi://pay mean?

It is the common UPI payment URI scheme used to open a payment request in compatible UPI apps.

Can I share the generated link on WhatsApp?

You can copy the link and share it, but whether it opens a UPI app depends on the payer's device, app, and platform behavior.

Does the link include my UPI PIN?

No. UPI PIN is never included in the link or QR. The payer enters their PIN only inside their UPI app.
